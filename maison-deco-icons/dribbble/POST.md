## Title

Maison: 24 Art Deco fine-dining icons

## Description

Fine dining, drawn the way the 1920s would have done it. Every outline in Maison is a hollow inline rail, the double gold line of grand hotel menus and Deco posters, finished with sunburst fans and solid gold accents.

What's inside:
• 24 icons in 4 courses: Service, Cellar, Delicacies, Kitchen
• Gold SVG with a champagne-gold gradient, made for emerald, black and oxblood grounds
• Mono SVG in one flat colour, recolourable, ready for foil stamping and print
• The open core is a true mask, so it stays transparent on any background
• Pure vector with no filters, so it opens cleanly in Figma, Illustrator and Sketch

Made for restaurants, hotels, bars, wine lists, menus, packaging and reservation apps.
