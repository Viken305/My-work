## Description (copy-paste)
Right now, dozens of volcanoes are erupting somewhere on Earth. But why does our planet explode at all? We dive to the core, melt a rock three different ways, follow the cracks in Earth's shell, find out why some volcanoes ooze while others explode, and discover why we owe volcanoes far more than we think.
