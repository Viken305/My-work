## Title

Calibre: 24 polished-chrome watchmaking icons

## Description

Haute horlogerie, in icon form. Every line in Calibre is a polished steel rod with a dark rim, a banded chrome body and a specular highlight, finished with heat-blued hands, ruby jewels and touches of gold.

What's inside:
• 24 icons in 4 groups: Timepieces, Movement, Complications, Atelier
• Chrome SVG: polished steel, blued steel, ruby and gold, built from gradients
• Line SVG in one flat colour, recolourable for any dial or brand
• Pure vector with no filters, so it opens cleanly in Figma, Illustrator and Sketch
• 200 px grid with consistent rod weights across the set

Made for watch boutiques, brands and retailers, collector apps, service centres and editorial.
