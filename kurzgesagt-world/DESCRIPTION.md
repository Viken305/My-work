## Description (copy-paste)
You've lived on this planet your whole life, but Earth is far stranger than it looks. Join Nimbus, a little cloud who drifts around the world, for seven facts that will change how you see home: more trees than stars, a lumpy planet, a runaway Moon, an icy desert, a desert that feeds a rainforest, the true majority of life, and an ocean floor we still haven't mapped.
