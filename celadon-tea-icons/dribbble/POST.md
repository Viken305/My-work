## Title

Celadon: 24 glazed-ceramic tea-ceremony icons

## Description

The tea ceremony, fired in the kiln. Every icon in Celadon is glazed like real pottery: celadon, tenmoku, shino, cobalt ruri, amber, iron and lacquer over speckled raw clay, with the glaze dripping where it stops and a soft gloss on top.

What's inside:
• 24 icons in 4 groups: Ceremony, Teaware, Leaf, Tea house
• Glazed SVG in full colour, eight glazes over speckled clay
• Line SVG in one colour, recolourable, with the same outlines, drips and brushwork
• Pure vector with no filters, so it opens cleanly in Figma, Illustrator and Sketch
• 200 px grid with consistent outlines across the set

Made for tea brands, matcha cafés, tea houses, packaging, menus and wellness apps.
