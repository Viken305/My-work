# The Deepest Hole Ever Dug: narration and fact sheet

Voice: Kokoro `af_heart` (US female, 1.05×). Narration lines live in `script.py`.

1. In 1989, a story spread around the world. Scientists had drilled so deep, they heard screams from hell.
   That part is a hoax. But the hole is real. And what they actually found down there is stranger.
2. This is the Kola Superdeep Borehole, in the Arctic north of Russia. Drilling began in 1970.
   The hole is only twenty three centimetres wide. About the size of a dinner plate.
   But it goes down twelve thousand, two hundred and sixty two metres.
3. Stack the Eiffel Tower thirty seven times, and you still would not reach the bottom.
   Drop Mount Everest in upside down, and its peak would hang three kilometres above the floor.
   It is deeper than the deepest point of the ocean. And it took almost twenty years to drill.
4. Almost seven kilometres down, they found fossils. Twenty four species of tiny plankton, around two billion years old.
   Deeper still, there was water. Not from any ocean, but squeezed out of the rock itself, and trapped.
   And the mud coming back up was boiling. Not only from heat, but because it was full of hydrogen gas.
5. At the bottom, the rock was a hundred and eighty degrees. Almost twice what they expected. It began to act less
   like stone, and more like hot plastic.
   So they stopped. After all of that, they had drilled through only a third of the crust. Not even a fifth of one
   percent of the way to the centre.
   Today, the deepest hole ever dug is hidden under a small, rusty metal cap.
   And no. It does not scream.
