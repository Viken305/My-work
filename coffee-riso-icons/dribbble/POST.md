## Title

Coffee Riso Icons: 24 two-ink risograph icons

## Description

A specialty-coffee icon set printed the risograph way, in two inks: Fluorescent Pink and Blue. Where the inks overlap they mix into a deep indigo, a halftone gives a lighter tint, and the pink layer sits a little off register like a real print run.

What's inside:
• 24 icons in 4 categories: Brewing, Drinks, Beans & origin, Café life
• Textured SVG with grain, halftone and misregistration, for websites, menus and print
• Clean SVG with flat fills and each ink on its own layer, ready for Figma, Illustrator and Sketch
• 200 px grid, 4 px round linework, consistent across the set

Made for cafés, roasters, coffee apps, menus and packaging.
