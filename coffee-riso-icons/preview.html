A two-ink risograph set for specialty coffee: Fluorescent Pink and Blue on uncoated paper, printed slightly out of register. Every icon ships as a textured SVG with grain and halftone, and a clean SVG of flat, editable shapes.

Textured files use SVG filters and blend modes, so they look right in browsers and on websites. Clean files use only flat fills and clip paths, so they open correctly in Figma, Illustrator and Sketch. Overlaps in the clean files are pre-mixed: blue over pink becomes deep indigo, blue over the halftone becomes mid blue.
