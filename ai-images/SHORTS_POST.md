**Description**
Type one sentence and an AI turns it into a picture in seconds. But every one of those pictures starts as pure static.

Here's what actually happens: your prompt is chopped into tokens and turned into numbers on a map of meaning. The model was trained on billions of captioned images to do one job: guess the noise in a noisy picture. To make a new image it starts from random static and removes a little noise at every step, steered by your words, on a tiny 64×64 version of the picture that a decoder then paints in full.

Same words + new static = a new picture. Same static = the exact same picture.

All pictures in this video are illustrations drawn in code, not AI output. Simplified: real models differ in the details.
