# Stars video: style guide (premium)

## 1. Look
The same flat-vector family as the earlier videos, pushed to a **premium, cinematic** finish:

| Technique | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Motion blur** | Every frame is the average of 4 sub-frames across half a frame's shutter, so fast moves smear naturally instead of strobing |
| **Bloom** | Bright light bleeds into its surroundings (multi-scale, only above a high threshold so shapes keep their form) |
| **Textured nebulae** | Procedural noise (fBm plus domain warp) colour-ramped into soft gas clouds, cached and layered with screen/additive blending |
| **Parallax depth** | 3 star layers, nebula layers and foreground bokeh move at different speeds with the camera |
| **Depth of field** | Out-of-focus foreground dust drifting past the lens |
| **Additive light** | Glows, plasma, jets and flares use additive blending, like real light |
| **Lens flare** | Anamorphic streak plus ghost discs on big flashes (ignition, supernova) |
| **Grade** | Gentle S-curve, teal shadows, warm highlights, vignette, film grain, subtle chromatic aberration |
| **Camera** | Slow push-ins, drifts, impact shake on explosions, zoom-throughs between scales |

## 2. Palette
| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Void / deep space | `#060716` → `#0E0C2C` |
| Nebula | violet `#8246DC`, magenta `#E83E8C`, peach `#FFBE8C`, teal `#28C8D2` |
| Young / Sun-like star | solar `#FFD678` with hot-white core |
| Red giant | `#FF603C` |
| White dwarf / neutron star | ice blue `#96BEFF` |
| Supernova | amber `#FFAA3C` + magenta + teal debris |
| Gold (finale) | `#FFC83C` |

Each chapter shifts the dominant light: **birth** = violet/magenta clouds, **life** = solar gold, **small death** = red then teal/pink nebula, **giant death** = amber flash into ice-blue, **star stuff** = the full palette returning, ending on Earth's blue.

## 3. ORBI (new guide character)
A tiny deep-space probe that reacts to everything we see.
- **Body:** pearl-white sphere with a seam and rivets, soft shading, **rim light that takes the colour of the scene's light** (gold near the Sun, red near the giant, blue near the pulsar).
- **Face:** dark glass visor with a reflection streak; **one big glowing eye** with six expressions: neutral, happy (^), amazed (wide), focused (slit), worried (low lid), sleepy.
- **Ears:** two solar panels that **flap up when excited and droop when worried**, with a moving specular glint.
- **Antenna:** springy, with a blinking magenta tip and follow-through when ORBI moves.
- **Thruster:** a light-plume under the body whose strength follows ORBI's speed.
- **Animation rules:** squash and stretch along the direction of travel, banking into turns, anticipation before big moves, overshoot when settling, recoil from explosions.
- **Mouth:** none, so ORBI never "speaks"; the narrator does.

## 4. Typography
Montserrat ExtraBold in sparse, wide-tracked labels. Element tiles (Fe, Ca, O, Au…) are the main on-screen text device.

## 5. Sound
The same procedural pad, plucks and SFX, scored darker and wider: low drones in the void, rising risers into ignition and collapse, a silent beat before the supernova, then a full swell.
