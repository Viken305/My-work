## Description (copy-paste)
The iron in your blood, the calcium in your bones, the gold in your jewellery: none of it was made on Earth. Join ORBI, a tiny deep-space probe, on a trip from the Big Bang to the first giant stars, through the birth, life and death of stars, to the explosions that scattered the ingredients of planets, and of you.
