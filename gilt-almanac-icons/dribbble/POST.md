## Title

Gilt Almanac: 24 engraved zodiac & celestial icons

## Description

An icon set engraved like the plates of an old star atlas. Every shadow is built from fine vector hatching, then finished in gold foil.

The twelve zodiac signs sit on engraved coins, and the hatching on each coin follows its element: fire at 45°, earth horizontal, air at 135°, water vertical. The other twelve icons are the night sky: sun, moons, eclipse, Saturn, comet, north star, constellation, telescope, astrolabe, armillary sphere and crystal ball.

What's inside:
• 24 icons: 12 zodiac, 12 celestial
• Gilt SVG with a gold-foil gradient, for dark backgrounds
• Ink SVG in one flat colour, recolourable, ready for foil stamping and letterpress
• Pure vector with no filters or raster textures, so it opens cleanly in Figma, Illustrator and Sketch
• 200 px grid, consistent line weights across the set

Made for astrology apps, tarot decks, candle and wellness brands, packaging and editorial.
