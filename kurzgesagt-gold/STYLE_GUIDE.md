# Style guide: How Earth Makes Gold (vertical Short)

## Format
- **9:16 native** (1080x1920, 30 fps), animated for the phone from the start: no cropping of a 16:9 film.
- **Length:** about 2:40 (under the 3-minute Shorts limit). Narration currently 1:45, plus visual beats.
- **Safe zones:** nothing important in the top 220 px (search/back buttons), the bottom 400 px (title, channel, music)
  or the right 150 px between 900–1520 px (like/comment/share). Big visuals live in the middle band.
- **Captions** burned in, word by word: Montserrat ExtraBold 70 px, white with the key word in gold, sitting just above
  the bottom safe zone.

## Look
Premium pipeline (same as the stars/immune/world videos): additive light, bloom, motion blur, grain, depth.
Every scene is built around one strong vertical composition: one hero object, centred, big.

## Palette
| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Space / night | navy `#0A0C24` → `#12143A` |
| Gold (hero material) | highlight `#FFE296`, body `#ECAA34`, shade `#C8801E`, deep `#6E3810` |
| Neutron stars | ice blue `#96C8FF` |
| Molten Earth | deep red `#782828` → orange `#DC6E28` → golden core |
| Quartz vein | warm white `#ECE8E0` |
| Rock | dusty mauve `#78565C` with strata bands |

Gold is always **metal**, never flat yellow: dark rim, warm body, a crisp window highlight and a coloured environment
reflection, so it reads as shiny and valuable.

## Character: AURI
A gold atom (79 protons) and the guide of the video.
- Polished gold ball with three tilted **electron orbits**; the electrons glow cyan and pass in front of and behind
  the body (real depth).
- Shared **animated face rig**: eyes, lids, brows and mouth morph between expressions with overshoot and blinks.
- Expressions: happy, shocked, worried, laugh, determined (with a **miner's hard hat** for the earthquake scene), smug, curious.
- The reflection band takes the colour of each scene (blue in space, red in the molten Earth, grey in the rock).

## Story beats (5 parts)
1. **Hook:** a gold ring floating in space; "older than the Sun".
2. **The forge:** two neutron stars spiral in and collide; a flash; gold nuggets fly outward.
3. **The lost gold:** the molten young Earth; gold drops sink to the core; a "4 m deep" golden layer wraps the planet.
4. **The delivery and the squeeze:** meteorites rain gold onto the young Earth; hot water carries it through cracks;
   an earthquake opens a crack, water flashes to steam, a gold-flecked quartz vein is left behind (Auri in a hard hat).
5. **The punchline:** all gold ever mined as one 22 m cube next to a house and a person; quick recap; end card.

## Typography
Montserrat ExtraBold. Title "HOW EARTH / MAKES GOLD" on the hook. Small pill chips label each new idea at the top of the
middle band (below the top safe zone).

## Sound
Procedural pad and plucks; a riser into the collision, a deep boom and shimmer on the kilonova; low rumbles in the
molten Earth; a sharp crack and hiss of steam for the earthquake; a bright chime on the final cube.
