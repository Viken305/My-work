**Title:** What 72 Hours Without Sleep Does to Your Body 😵 #shorts

(Alternatives: "We Kept Sam Awake for 72 Hours. Then the Brain Started Lying." · "Your Body After 24, 48 and 72 Hours Without Sleep")

**Description (YouTube):**

What happens if you stay awake for 3 days? We kept Sam up for 72 hours to find out. 👁️⏰

⏱️ Hour 0: a chemical called adenosine starts filling your brain's "sleep pressure jar". Coffee only hides it.
🌙 4 a.m.: your body clock hits rock bottom. Body temperature drops, the urge to sleep peaks.
🍸 Hour 24: your reaction time is as bad as someone with 0.10% blood alcohol, over the legal driving limit.
😤 Your brain's emotional alarm, the amygdala, becomes 60% more reactive.
🧠 Your memory center takes in about 40% less new information.
🍩 Hunger hormones flip: ghrelin up, leptin down. Hello, junk food.
🧹 One sleepless night raised beta-amyloid, a waste protein linked to Alzheimer's, by about 5%.
⚫ Hour 48: microsleeps. Tiny blackouts, even with your eyes open. 3 seconds at highway speed ≈ a football field.
👻 Hour 72: many people start to hallucinate.
🏆 The record: Randy Gardner, 264 hours awake, in 1964.

The good news: a night or two of deep sleep resets you. Most adults need 7–9 hours. Remember the jar. 🫙

⚠️ Don't try this. Sleep loss is dangerous, especially behind the wheel.
