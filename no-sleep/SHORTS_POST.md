**Title:** What 72 Hours Without Sleep Does to Your Body #shorts

**Description:**
Stay awake for three days and your brain starts showing you things that aren't there. 👁️

Hour by hour with Sam: at 24 hours you react like someone over the drink-drive limit, your emotional alarm gets 60 % more
reactive and your hunger hormones flip. By 48 hours your brain takes microsleeps, tiny blackouts you won't notice, even
with your eyes open. By 72 hours, many people hallucinate. 😵‍💫

The good news: a night or two of deep sleep resets you. Remember the jar. 🫙

Follow @BrighterMinds for more animated science that makes your brain a little brighter. ✨

- Dawson & Reid, Nature 1997 (24 h awake ≈ 0.10 % BAC)

- Poudel et al., NeuroImage 2014 (microsleeps)
- Waters et al., Frontiers in Psychiatry 2018 (hallucinations after ~72 h)

Don't try this. Sleep loss is dangerous, especially behind the wheel.
