# 72 Hours Without Sleep: style guide

A 9:16 Short (2:16) rendered entirely in code. The style is **"Night Shift" motion graphics**: one character, Sam, and
one long night. A clock HUD and a 0–72 h timeline sit at the top of every shot, so viewers always know where they are.

## The picture gets tired too
| Act | Hours | Colour | Post |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–24 | midnight blue, cyan `#7FD4FF` | clean, light grain |
| 2 | 24–48 | sodium-lamp amber `#F2A93B` | more grain, slight chromatic aberration |
| 3 | 48–72 | alarm red `#FF4D6D` | heavy grain, the frame wobbles (noise displacement), red/cyan split; microsleeps drop the picture to black |
| Recovery | after 72 | blue again | everything snaps back clean |

## Cast and props
- **Sam:** lavender hoodie `#C9C3FF`, dot eyes. Rig controls eyelids, dark circles, staring eyes, mouth, slump, an arm
  that holds a mug or a donut, and an x-ray line version (silhouette, ribs, spine, glowing heart).
- **The adenosine jar:** the running visual gag. It fills with every waking hour, coffee wraps it in a CAFFEINE sleeve
  that hides the level, it is nearly full at 72 h, and it drains during recovery sleep.
- Rooms, an alarm clock, a cocktail glass and a BAC scale, a side-view brain (dimming prefrontal cortex, glowing
  amygdala), hormone meters, an open fridge, a top-down night road with a football field to scale, a shadow figure,
  a sepia record card, and a sleep-stage graph (hypnogram).

## Typography
- **Big Shoulders Display Black** for the hour slams, titles and emphasis words.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (designed for low-vision readers, fitting for tired eyes) for captions.
- **Martian Mono** for the HUD, labels and numbers.

## Captions
Word-synced from the real audio (same system as the other Shorts), 88 px Atkinson Bold with a dark outline and a soft
shadow so they read over any picture. The spoken word pops (a quick 10 % scale spring); emphasis words switch to the
condensed display face in upper case, in the act's accent colour, with a glow and a bigger pop. Max 3 words per phrase, kept above y = 1480 so the
Shorts interface never covers them. In the hallucination they are drawn after the post pass, so they stay readable
while the picture warps.

## Motion and sound
- Each milestone (24, 36, 48, 72) is a full-screen number slam with a riser, trailer hit and sub drop.
- Scenes change with an eyelid blink wipe. The ending blinks back into the opening eye, so the Short loops.
- A clock tick runs under the whole film; it races in the hook and goes off-beat at 72 h.
- Music is a lo-fi synth pad that detunes a little more each act, then resolves to a clean "wonder" pad in recovery.
- During microsleeps music and effects cut out completely; only the voice remains.
