## Description (YouTube)
Look at a map and borders seem random: some wiggle, some run ruler straight, and some are just strange. But every shape is a story. We fly around a 3D globe to find out why. 🌍

🌊 NATURE (0:09): the Rio Grande (and the time it moved, El Chamizal), the Pyrenees, and why Chile is so long and thin
📏 STRAIGHT LINES (0:47): the 49th parallel (1818), Point Roberts, Africa's colonial lines, and Bir Tawil, the desert neither Egypt nor Sudan claims
🤝 DEALS (1:32): Namibia's Caprivi Strip and Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor
🏠 ACCIDENTS (1:53): Baarle, where your front door decides your country
