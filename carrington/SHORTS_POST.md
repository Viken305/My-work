## Title
The Day the Sun Hit Earth: The Carrington Event #shorts

## Description
In September 1859, the Sun hit Earth with the biggest solar storm ever recorded. Astronomer Richard Carrington saw the
flare at 11:18 a.m. Just 17.6 hours later, the cloud it threw slammed into Earth's magnetic field. Auroras blazed as far
south as Cuba, Hawaii and Panama, gold miners in the Rockies got up to make breakfast at 1 a.m., and telegraph
operators sent messages with their batteries unplugged, powered by the sky alone. In July 2012, a storm just as strong
missed us by about nine days. ☀️⚡
