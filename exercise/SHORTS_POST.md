## Description
Hit play on a workout and every organ has to hit its notes. 🎮

⚡ LV1 IGNITION: adrenaline floods in, and your muscles' share of blood jumps from ~20% to ~80%
🫁 LV2 AIR: 12 → 40+ breaths a minute, 6 → ~100 litres of air a minute
🔋 LV3 FUEL: ATP lasts only seconds, so sugar and fat recharge it nonstop (plus the burn)
❤️ LV4 MAX HEART: ~180 BPM, pumping up to 20 L of blood a minute (4×!)
🔥 LV5 HEAT: ~75% of muscle energy becomes heat, and you sweat 1+ litre an hour
⭐ BONUS: endorphins + endocannabinoids (the runner's high), the afterburn, and a stronger heart

Which level hits hardest for you? 👇
