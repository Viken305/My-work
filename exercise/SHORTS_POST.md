## Description (YouTube)
The moment you start exercising, your body turns into a rhythm game. 🎮 Every organ has to hit its notes, right on time. Here's what happens inside, level by level:

⚡ LV1 IGNITION: adrenaline floods your blood, and your muscles' share of blood jumps from ~20% to ~80%
🫁 LV2 AIR: breathing goes from 12 to 40+ breaths a minute, and air flow from 6 to ~100 litres a minute
🔋 LV3 FUEL: muscles store only seconds of ATP, so sugar and fat recharge it nonstop. When oxygen can't keep up, acid builds: the burn
❤️ LV4 MAX HEART: around 180 BPM, pumping up to 20 L of blood a minute (4× more than at rest)
🔥 LV5 HEAT: about 75% of muscle energy turns into heat, so you can sweat 1+ litre an hour
⭐ BONUS ROUND: endorphins and endocannabinoids (the runner's high), the afterburn, and a stronger heart that needs fewer beats at rest

Level complete. Hit play again tomorrow. 🔁
Which level hits hardest for you? 👇

Sources: Guyton & Hall, Textbook of Medical Physiology · McArdle, Katch & Katch, Exercise Physiology · Robergs et al. 2004 (Am J Physiol) · Fuss et al. 2015 (PNAS) · Siebers et al. 2021 (Psychoneuroendocrinology) · Børsheim & Bahr 2003 (Sports Med).
Numbers are typical values; max heart rate is roughly 220 minus your age. Educational content, not medical advice.
