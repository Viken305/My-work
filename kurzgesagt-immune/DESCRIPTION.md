## Description (copy-paste)
Right now, as you watch this, you are under attack. Meet Kai, a killer T cell, and the army of nearly two trillion cells that fights a war inside you every day: the walls that keep invaders out, the first responders that rush to a wound, the messenger that raises the alarm, the special forces that hunt infected cells, and the memory that makes vaccines work.
